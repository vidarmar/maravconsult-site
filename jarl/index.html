Strength after 50

Jarl is a calm, no-nonsense strength programme for men over 50. Full-body trainings that build and keep muscle, with joint-friendly exercise swaps, plain explanations and recovery that respects your age.

What Jarl does

  • Full-body strength trainings 2 to 6 days a week, in 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes.
  • Joint-friendly swaps for knees, shoulders and lower back, and a short check-in before each training.
  • An illustrated how-to for every exercise, plus a guided mobility routine for rest days.
  • Light days when you train two days in a row, so you recover properly.
  • Heart rate from Apple Watch, Garmin or a Bluetooth chest strap, and guidance by breathing and effort if your medication lowers your heart rate.
  • Progress tracking with age-group benchmarks, so you can see where you stand.

Jarl Premium

Starts with 14 days free. Choose yearly or monthly; prices are shown in your local currency in the app. No account, and your data stays on your iPhone.

Support · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Jarl is a training guide, not medical advice. If you have heart problems, high blood pressure or recent surgery, check with your doctor before you start.
