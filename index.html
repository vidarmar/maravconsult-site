01 Weight & Balance consultancy

Load control that stands up to audit.

Hands-on support for operators, handlers and airports implementing or improving weight and balance and departure control processes.

  • Departure control system (DCS) implementation and configuration
  • Load sheet integration — DCS, flight planning, crew and reservation systems
  • Aircraft weight and balance data, CG envelopes and trim calculations
  • EFB regulatory compliance and documentation
  • Operational engineering calculations and verification
  • UAT planning, test execution and acceptance reporting
  • Technical writing, specifications and bid support
02 Training application development

Training apps people come back to.

Design and development of training applications — from operational user training for aviation software to consumer strength-training apps on iOS.

  • Online user training to replace slide-based classroom sessions
  • Interactive, scenario-based modules for operational systems
  • iOS app design and development, from prototype to App Store
  • Apple Health integration, progress tracking and subscriptions
  • Clear, accessible design for specific audiences
  • Rapid clickable prototypes to validate ideas early

About

Vidar Thor Marisson

Marisson Aviation Consulting is an independent practice based in Kristiansand, Norway. It brings around a decade of experience in aviation IT project delivery, user acceptance testing and technical writing to operators, handlers and airports.

The work sits where operations meet software: departure control, weight and balance, load sheets and EFB compliance — and increasingly, the training tools that help people use those systems well.
