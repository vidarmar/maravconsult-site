Daylight and Apple Health

How do I connect Apple Health?

In Lumi, tap the place name at the top to open Settings, then tap Connect Apple Health and Allow. On Apple’s screen, turn on Time in Daylight and Sleep, then tap Allow. You can change this later in the iPhone Settings app under Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Lumi.

Why are my daylight minutes empty?

Time in Daylight is measured by Apple Watch SE (2nd generation) or Series 6 and later, with watchOS 10 and iOS 17 or later. The watch has to be worn outdoors, and the minutes appear after it syncs. Without a watch, tap +10 min manually on the Today tab after a walk.

Where does my sleep come from?

Lumi reads sleep that Apple Watch or the Sleep schedule in the Health app records. You can also add a night yourself in the sleep journal under Rhythm. Your own entry is used when both exist.

Why are the sun times different from the weather app?

Lumi calculates sunrise and sunset for a flat horizon at your position. Mountains, buildings and clouds can make the sun appear later and disappear earlier where you are.

Reminders

My reminders don’t arrive

Check that reminders are on in Lumi under Rhythm, and in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications, then Lumi. Focus modes can also hold notifications back. After the free week, reminders continue with Lumi Plus.

How do I change the times?

Go to Rhythm. Use − and + to move each reminder by 15 minutes, and the switch to turn it on or off. The lamp reminder follows your wake time.

Can Lumi tell me how much vitamin D to take?

No. Lumi only reminds you. Ask your doctor or pharmacist what suits you.

Location

Do I have to share my location?

No. You can pick the nearest place from the list in Settings instead. If you use your location, Lumi rounds it to about 100 metres and keeps it on your iPhone.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, with a copy in your own iCloud if you’re signed in. Lumi has no accounts or servers. Read the Privacy Policy.

I got a new iPhone. Where is my journal?

Sign in to the same iCloud account and install Lumi. Your settings and journal come back from iCloud the first time you open the app.

How do I delete my data?

In Lumi, open Settings and tap Delete my data twice. This clears the app and the iCloud copy.

Lumi Plus

How does the free week work?

Everything in Lumi is open for 7 days from the day you set it up, with no payment details. After that, the sun curve, walk window and daylight minutes stay free. Choose Lumi Plus, yearly or monthly, to keep the rest. Prices are shown in your local currency in the app.

I already subscribe. Why does Lumi ask me again?

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, open Settings in Lumi and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Lumi, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
