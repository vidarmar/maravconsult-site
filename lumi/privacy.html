LumiWinter Light Routine

Privacy Policy

Your data stays with you

Version 1.0 · Effective 3 November 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. Lumi doesn’t know who you are.
  • Your journal and settings stay on your iPhone, with a copy in your own iCloud if you use iCloud. We never receive them.
  • Apple Health data is read on your iPhone and never leaves it. It isn’t stored, uploaded, shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Your location is used only on your iPhone to work out sunrise, sunset and daylight.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. The app has no servers of its own.

1. Who we are

Lumi: Winter Light Routine (“Lumi”, “the app”) is made by Marisson Aviation Consulting, a sole proprietorship (enkeltpersonforetak) registered in Norway, organisation number 917 105 375 MVA, Maristien 7B, 4638 Kristiansand, Norway (“we”, “us”). Contact: vidar@maravconsult.com.

This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. It applies to the iPhone app and to these web pages.

2. Information stored on your iPhone

To work, Lumi stores the following in the app’s own storage on your iPhone:

  • Your settings: language, the place you chose or your approximate location (rounded to about 100 metres), daily daylight goal, wake time, reminder times and which reminders are on.
  • What you log in the app: sleep journal entries, mood and energy check-ins, light lamp sessions and daylight minutes you add yourself.
  • When you started using the app, so it knows when the free week ends.

We don’t receive or see any of this.

3. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Lumi keeps a copy of the information in section 2 in your private iCloud key-value storage. This lets a new iPhone, or a reinstalled app, pick up where you left off. The copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple’s terms. We have no access to it. Apple Health data is never stored in iCloud by Lumi.

4. Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Lumi asks your permission to read:

  • Time in Daylight (measured by Apple Watch)
  • Sleep

Lumi reads this data on your iPhone each time you open the app, to show your daylight minutes, your sleep and your wake times. Lumi:

  • does not write anything to Apple Health,
  • does not save Apple Health data in its own storage or in iCloud,
  • does not send Apple Health data to us or to anyone else,
  • does not use Apple Health data for advertising, marketing or data mining.

Connecting is optional. You can change or withdraw access at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Lumi.

5. Location

If you choose “Use my location”, Lumi asks for your location while you use the app. It uses it on your iPhone to calculate sunrise, sunset, daylight length and the best time to get outside. Lumi rounds the position to about 100 metres before saving it, and never sends it to us. You can instead pick a place from a list, or turn off location access in Settings, then Lumi, then Location.

6. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules and shows itself. No server is involved. You can turn them off in the app under Rhythm, or in Settings, then Notifications, then Lumi.

7. Purchases

Lumi Plus subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, card details or Apple ID. Apple provides us with general sales reports that do not identify you. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.

8. Who else is involved

  • Apple distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight, stores your iCloud copy, and may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example crash reports if you have turned on sharing with app developers. If you test the app in TestFlight and send feedback, Apple passes that feedback, and any screenshot you attach, to us.
  • GitHub hosts these web pages. GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses when you visit, under GitHub’s privacy statement. The app itself does not contact GitHub.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and its typefaces are built in, so it makes no requests to third-party services. Links in the app to health information sites, such as helsenorge.no, open in your web browser.

9. Legal basis and your rights

Because we don’t collect or receive your personal data, we don’t process it on our side. The data described in sections 2 to 5 is processed on your own device and in your own iCloud account, under your control.

You can delete all of Lumi’s data at any time:

  • In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my data twice. This also clears the iCloud copy.
  • Or delete the app from your iPhone. The iCloud copy can then be removed in Settings, then your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete the correspondence when it’s no longer needed. You have the right to access, correct and delete that information. If you believe we have handled your personal data incorrectly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), or the authority where you live.

10. Age

Lumi is intended for people aged 16 and over.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, for example when we add optional calendar, weather or northern lights features, we will update this policy, change the version and date above, and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
